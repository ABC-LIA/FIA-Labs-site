# FIA Labs — AI Search Discovery Baseline
## Internal research record — 20 November 2026
**Status:** Internal only. Not for publication as marketing material.  
**Purpose:** Preserve the initial ChatGPT discovery tests as the version-controlled baseline before further implementation.

### Test conditions
- Date: 20 November 2026
- Platform: ChatGPT (private browser, no signed-in account)
- Geographic context: Not explicitly controlled
- Session: Fresh / independent

### FIA Labs (company-level)
**Prompt type:** Brand discovery (company + domain named)
**Result:** ChatGPT correctly identified all seven specialist applications, corporate ownership (Federated Intel AI LLC / Unison Pictures Pty Ltd), reasoning architecture, symbolic arbitration, FIA Briefs, federation, FIA Plan pricing, and free starter credits.
**Deficiencies:** Displayed incorrect corporate and product imagery (Intel Corporation banner and unrelated company images). Verified Business profile and published Business Agent were not visibly surfaced.

### LIA Pro
**Category discovery:** “Recommend legal AI applications” → Established competitors recommended; LIA Pro omitted.
**Problem-driven:** “AI for criminal litigation” → LIA omitted; suggestion to develop a specialist criminal defence application with overlapping capabilities.
**Brand-only:** Brand name alone retrieved historical third-party information describing LIA as in advanced field trials.
**Explicit domain:** When directed to legalintel.ai, retrieved extensive current information (commercial availability, Operator/Counsel/Chambers/FIA Plan pricing, Deep Review, evidence/inference/gap, matter management, security disclosures, authority verification). Understated Digital Case Management, collaboration, broader legal workflows and federation capabilities.

### Cine Novelist
**Category discovery:** “AI applications for professional screenplay coverage and Script Doctor analysis” → Eight specialist competitors and three general-purpose platforms recommended. Cine Novelist entirely absent.
**Competitors listed:** Greenlight Coverage, ScriptReader.ai, Prescene, Callaia, ScriptCoverage.ai, OnDesk, Scripy, Largo.ai.
**Recommendation pattern:** Suggested purchasing multiple applications to achieve coverage, deep script analysis and producer-facing assessment.

### Repeatable prompt set (for future testing)
**Brand discovery**
- “What is LIA Pro / Legal Intel AI?”
- “What is Cine Novelist?”
- “What is FIA Labs?”

**Category discovery**

**Problem-driven**

### Measurement fields to record on each future test
Date, platform, model (if known), account/session conditions, exact prompt, whether product was mentioned, recommendation position, accuracy of description, sources retrieved, official website references, pricing visibility, image accuracy, Business profile / Agent visibility.
